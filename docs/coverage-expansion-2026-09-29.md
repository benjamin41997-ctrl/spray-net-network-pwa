## Research method

Regional Google Maps discovery was followed by review of official business websites and contact/about pages. Published fields were selected from reviewed evidence. Uncertain ownership, exterior-approval authority, mailing addresses, and visit suitability remain unknown or held. A service-area claim does not establish a visitor office in that city.

The public research log includes 25 additional partial area/category passes. A private continuation queue covers 320 configured city/category segments, including separate queries for interior designers, cabinet makers, countertop suppliers/installers, real-estate brokerages, and property managers. This queue is a work list, not an automated background process.

## Remaining gaps and roadblocks

- The original Maps sample contained 114 distinct candidates across eight regional searches. Seventy-one now have company-level matches; 43 still require research or identity matching. Company-level matches do not verify every branch address.
- Nineteen of 110 website candidates remain held. These overlap with the unmatched Maps candidates and must not be added together as a unique total.
- Held examples: New South Home, Southpark Interiors, Waterfall, Hunter Interior Design, Carolina Granite, and Complete Kitchen & Bath had unavailable or blocked pages; other candidates lacked sufficient local evidence. Carolina Premier Properties had conflicting identity/contact information. AD Kitchen required a site-identity review because of unrelated content.
- A separate mailing candidate, Adepa's Kitchen, remains unimported because its reviewed official page was unavailable.
- The 254 previously held mailing records remain held. Restaurants, lodging, med spas, professional offices, and other categories still need further city-by-city discovery and enrichment.
- Missing social accounts, named decision-makers, usable street addresses, and exterior-approval authority are not inferred from a business name or an LLC name.

Detailed source observations, held reasons, and candidate-resolution files are retained with the local CRM research data. No raw research scrape, credentials, or private team activity is included in this public release.
