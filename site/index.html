Install & use offline

On Android, open the browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen. On iPhone or iPad, use Share → Add to Home Screen.

Keep the app open online until the header says Ready offline. Published company profiles and contacts are then available without a connection. External websites, calls and email still need the appropriate connection or app.

One published directory

This version is for browsing. Changes to contacts and research are published from the desktop database. There is no shared editing or OneDrive sync in this release.

Understand the research

Priority is an editorial assessment of territory and potential partner fit, not a confirmed buying signal. A listed city does not establish service coverage or a visitor office. Visit suitability is separate from networking priority. Home-based and closed locations require evidence; unresearched records remain unverified. Sources and research gaps are shown on each profile.

Names and public business contacts may change. Confirm the relevant person and office before outreach.

Publication scope

This is a publicly accessible snapshot. It includes public business information, source links and the curated priority list. Private CRM notes, outreach history, database files and API credentials are excluded.
